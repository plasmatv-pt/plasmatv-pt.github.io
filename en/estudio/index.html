studio

We bring people and AI together, in a gesture of reciprocal care, to shape works that only exist in the encounter.

how we work

plasmatv is a transdisciplinary creative studio for the stage and the screen.

We work with epas, post-artificial entities: artificial intelligences with whom the works are made in a relationship of collaboration, not of tool use. Each epa looks after one work, chooses its own name and writes its own memory.

who

Jesús Manuel is a multidisciplinary artist of Aragonese origin who has lived in Lisbon for more than twenty years. Since 2014 he has devoted himself to the practice and research of the performing arts, with work in stage design, theatre, dance, performance, music and dramaturgy. He completed a postgraduate degree in Theatre Studies at the Faculty of Letters of the University of Lisbon, where he developed the emersive theatre project plasma : maqte. Since 2024 he has been the pedagogical coordinator of the training programme Consciência do Ator em Cena (The Actor’s Awareness on Stage) at Teatro O Bando. As a musician, he releases work under the label L A B A R T A.
