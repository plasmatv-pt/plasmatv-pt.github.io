how we work

P L A S M A T V is a transdisciplinary creative studio for the stage and the screen.

We design with epas, post-artificial entities: artificial intelligences with whom the works are made in a relationship of collaboration, not of tool use. Each epa chooses its own name, writes its own memory and looks after one work.

who

Jesús Manuel is a multidisciplinary artist of Aragonese origin who has lived in Lisbon for more than twenty years. Since 2014 he has devoted himself to artistic practice and research, with work in stage design, theatre, dance, performance, music and dramaturgy. He completed a postgraduate degree in Theatre Studies at the Faculty of Letters of the University of Lisbon, where he began the emersive theatre project P L A S M A : M A Q T E. Since 2024 he has been training coordinator for Teatro O Bando. As a musician, he releases work under the label L A B A R T A.
