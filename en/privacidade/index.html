privacy

This site does not use cookies and does not measure visits.

If you turn the sound off, the site keeps that choice in the browser tab’s session memory until you close the tab. It stores nothing else on your device.

The contact page sends your message through the studio’s hosting server, at dominios.pt (the same one where our email lives), straight to contacto@plasmatv.pt. The server does not store the message: it delivers it to the mailbox. To stop abuse, it keeps an irreversible digest of the sender’s IP address for one hour, and then deletes it. Messages are used only to reply to you.

Fonts and images are served by the site itself, with no requests to other services.

Like every server, the server hosting this site logs the requests it receives (the IP address, the page requested and the time). We do not use those logs for anything.
